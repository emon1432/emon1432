Executive Summary & Overview

A Khayer International is a comprehensive internal enterprise ERP and financial governance system designed specifically for a licensed overseas manpower recruitment agency (RL 2235). It coordinates candidate processing pipelines, medical clearances, visa issuance, foreign employer demands, and multi-currency accounting ledgers.

Business Context & Challenge

International recruitment involves multi-step immigration workflows, hundreds of local field agents, fluctuating foreign exchange receipts, and stringent government compliance guidelines. Spreadsheets cause tracking blind spots and agent commission discrepancies.

Engineered Solution & Architecture

Built a multi-module ERP featuring candidate lifecycle tracking from interview to flight departure. The financial engine maintains double-entry bookkeeping, agent commission balances, sub-agent deposits, employer invoices, and exportable government audit reports.

Core Functional Capabilities

  • End-to-end candidate lifecycle tracking: passport, medical, visa, and flight ticketing
  • Sub-agent and broker account ledgers with automated commission calculations
  • Multi-currency banking and cash ledger supporting BDT, USD, SAR, and AED
  • Visa quota allocation and foreign employer demand order management
  • Automated PDF candidate dossiers, flight manifests, and embassy documentation
  • Audit-ready financial reports, balance sheets, and profit/loss statements
