Executive Summary & Overview

Digital Store BD is an e-commerce platform and retail distributor in Bangladesh specializing in domestic and commercial RO/UV water purifiers, replacement filter cartridges, and spare parts. It provides customers with direct online purchasing, cash on delivery, and doorstep installation services.

Business Context & Challenge

Water purifier consumers need clarity regarding filtration stages, water source suitability (deep tube-well vs municipal), cartridge replacement timelines, and installation support.

Engineered Solution & Architecture

Built a specialized e-commerce catalog clearly categorizing systems by filtration stages (5-stage, 6-stage, mineral RO), featuring filter replacement schedules and doorstep installation booking.

Core Functional Capabilities

  • Comprehensive catalog of domestic, industrial, and commercial RO/UV purifiers
  • Replacement filter cartridges, sediment filters, RO membranes, and fitting parts catalog
  • Nationwide cash on delivery ordering with doorstep installation service options
  • Filter maintenance schedule guidelines and compatibility lookup for various purifier models
  • Customer product reviews, warranty registration, and technical support hotline
  • Admin inventory management with order dispatch and service technician scheduling
