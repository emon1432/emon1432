Executive Summary & Overview

Khidmah Hospital is the official healthcare digital portal and patient service system for Khidmah Hospital in Dhaka. It connects patients with medical consultants, diagnostic test catalogs, department schedules, and online appointment booking.

Business Context & Challenge

Patients often struggle to find doctor consultation schedules, current diagnostic pricing, or confirm appointments without making long phone calls or waiting in hospital lines.

Engineered Solution & Architecture

Designed a modern patient-centric healthcare portal that provides searchable consultant directories, departmental service overviews, transparent diagnostic test fee schedules, and an appointment booking engine.

Core Functional Capabilities

  • Specialized doctor directory filterable by medical department, gender, and availability
  • Online outpatient consultation appointment booking with confirmation email notifications
  • Comprehensive diagnostic test pricing catalog and preparation guidelines
  • Departmental clinical profiles covering Cardiology, Gynecology, Surgery, and Pediatrics
  • Hospital career portal, health checkup package promotions, and emergency contact details
  • Administrative patient queue tracking and doctor chamber schedule management
